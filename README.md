# 🌟 ZephyrSphere — 3D Aerial Sphere Shooter

**ZephyrSphere** is a modern, high-performance 3D arcade sphere-shooter built with Three.js, featuring interactive 3D architectural biomes, dynamic sky cycles, real-time physics, haptic touch controls, and progressive session storage.

## 🎮 Features

* **12 Interactive 3D Biomes**: Fully modeled procedural environments including *Royal Sanctum, Cyberpunk Drift, Angelic Cloud, Pastel Lagoon, Lo-Fi Cafe, Gothic Balcony, Gothic Castle, Enchanted Forest, Cyberpunk Terrace, Sandstone Desert, Abyssal Reef,* and *Alpine Meadows*.
* **Dynamic Sky & Lighting Engine**: Radiant Day, Starlight Night, and real-time Sky Cycles accelerated or decelerated with `W` and `S` keys.
* **Dual Sphere Arsenal**: Switch between iridescent bubbles and sports balls (Football, Basketball, Cricket, Tennis, Volleyball).
* **Gravity Avalanche Mechanics**: When clusters are popped, any lower-level spheres that lose ceiling connectivity immediately detach and fall with full gravity physics.
* **Haptic Touch & Precision Aiming**: Supports smooth mouse drag-aiming, range slider touch-aiming with haptic vibration, and double-tap-to-fire on mobile/tablet devices.
* **Live Pause Menu**: Pause anytime to store progress, review telemetry, or switch live 3D biomes and sky lighting.
* **Win/Lose & Rematch System**: Automatic detection for clearing the arena (Win) or reaching the bottom danger line (Lose), with immediate rematch and homescreen options.
* **Individual UI Hiding & Theme Switching**: Smooth blurred upper-left plague wave transitions between dark and light modes, with individual panel collapsing and restoration.
